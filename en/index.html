Research interests

  • Language universals and typology: what all human languages share, and how they differ
  • Cross-linguistic semantics, with formal semantics as the theory and computational methods as the tools
  • Measuring similarity between languages and dialects (phonology, lexicon)
  • Computational and phylogenetic linguistics
  • Writing systems and multilingual computing (input methods, internationalization)

Current work

Quantifying cross-linguistic similarity. Using phoneme inventories (PHOIBLE) and vocabulary data, I measure how close languages and dialects are, starting with the Romance languages. The aim is a measure that works for many languages, rests on explicit statistics, and can inform where a dialect ends and a language begins.
